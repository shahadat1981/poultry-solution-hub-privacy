How to request deletion

  1. Use the email button below, or send a message to poultrysolutionhub@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject Account and Data Deletion Request — Poultry Solution Hub.
  3. Identify the Google email linked to your Poultry Solution Hub account, if available. If you know the app's Firebase Account UID, you may include it. If you no longer have the app, you can still request deletion by email.
  4. State clearly that you want your Poultry Solution Hub account and associated personal information deleted.
  5. Our support team will contact you to verify account ownership and process the request. Sending a request does not delete your account instantly.

What information is covered?

An approved deletion request covers the Poultry Solution Hub app account and personal information associated with that account, including applicable profile and authentication information and personal records within connected app modules such as Marketplace, DOC Booking, Equipment Shop and Doctor services.

Some transaction, dispute-prevention, fraud-prevention or legally required records may need to be retained where justified. We will explain any such retention during processing and in the applicable privacy policy. Deactivating an account without deleting its associated personal information is not the deletion process.

Data kept only on your phone is not automatically removed through a cloud-account deletion request. If you wish to clear local records, use your device's app-storage settings after deciding whether you need any local backup. Previously created backups may also need to be removed separately.

Security: Never share passwords, one-time codes, Google ID tokens or financial account credentials in a deletion request.
